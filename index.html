WHY THIS MATTERS

She is becoming.
The world is already speaking.

Every girl is becoming a woman. The question is: who is helping shape who she becomes?

The world is already telling girls who they should be, what they should value and what success should look like. We believe she deserves another voice — one that helps her understand who she is, why she is here, how she is designed and what she can become.

A girl who knows her worth is better equipped to navigate a world that will constantly try to define it for her.

THE SEVEN THINGS

Foundations for becoming.

Seven foundations to help girls understand who they are, how they are designed and how to move toward womanhood with purpose.

01
FOUNDATION

Know Your Origin. Build on Truth.

The facts of your upbringing do not cancel the truth of your origin.

02
FORMULATION

Embrace Your Design. Own Your Difference.

Your distinction in life begins with the uniqueness of your design.

03
FRUSTRATION

Find Your Purpose. Build Your Confidence.

The discovery of your purpose gives you confidence to become who you were created to be.

04
FIXATION

Master Yourself. Make Better Choices.

The mastery of yourself gives direction to your emotions, choices and actions.

05
FORMATION

See Your Future. Shape Your Actions.

A clear vision gives your life direction and your daily choices discipline.

06
FORTIFICATION

Keep Growing. Keep Becoming.

Your capacity to grow determines the value you are able to create.

07
FINISHING

Stay Committed. Leave a Legacy.

The consistency of your commitment determines the legacy your life will leave.

You have seen the framework. The book carries the full journey.

THE WOMAN BEHIND THE PROJECT

Ruth Obi-Egbe

Ruth is a development catalyst, people-oriented change leader, author and family advocate passionate about helping people, organisations and families navigate growth and change without losing their foundation.

Through The Sugar & Spice Project, she is turning years of conversations, teaching and lived experience into resources that help girls build strong foundations for womanhood.
